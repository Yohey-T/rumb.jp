KohKey Line

About this line

KohKey Line is a phone number for the morning you take a step. Call it to hear an excerpt of “Out of Step,” the first track from the EP KohKey by Yohei Tsukasaki (out January 2027), and to leave a few words for someone taking a step tomorrow.

01This is not a helpline

No one answers this line, and messages are not checked in real time. If you need someone to talk to, visit findahelpline.com to find free, confidential support in your country. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

02Recording

On the phone, the line tells you that it records messages and how they are used. Pressing 2 to leave a message means you agree to be recorded.

On this site, you check a box to agree before your browser asks to use the microphone. You can play your message back and discard it before you send it.

Messages can be up to 30 seconds long, in any language.

03Who hears it

Only Yohei Tsukasaki, the person who made this record, listens to the messages. They are not published, shared, or used in the music. If that ever changes, this page and the line will say so before anyone records.

Messages are kept in private storage that only he can access. Your phone number is never published or shared.

04Stay anonymous

  • Please don’t say your name.
  • Please don’t include a phone number, address, or anything else that could identify you or someone else.

If a message includes details like these, it will be deleted.

0518 and over

Anyone can call and listen. You must be 18 or older to leave a message. Messages that seem to come from someone younger will be deleted.

06Removal requests

If you left a message and want it removed, write to:

yohey@rumb.jp

Please include the date and approximate time you called or recorded (with your time zone), whether you used the phone or this site, and the language you spoke. You don’t need to give a reason or your name. The message will be deleted within 14 days, and you will get a reply when it is done.

07Calling cost

The line is a US phone number. Your carrier’s normal rates apply, including international rates if you call from outside the US. Calling from this site is free.
