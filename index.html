Rumb

Yohei Tsukasaki

Composer  ·  Arranger  ·  Mix Engineer

"Life isn't about waiting for the storm to pass.
It's about learning to make music in the rain."

Rumb is the solo project of Japanese composer, arranger and mix engineer Yohei Tsukasaki. Drawing on over two decades of studio work, he handles every stage himself — from the first sound to the final master. Past releases include the album “pop calendar” (Teichiku Records) and digital releases via Zankyo Record.
